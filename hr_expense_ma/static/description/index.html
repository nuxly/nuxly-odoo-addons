HR Expense Mileage

Official mileage allowance for Odoo employee expenses

Let employees declare business trips made with their personal vehicle, and let Odoo compute the reimbursement automatically from an official mileage allowance scale.

Key Features

Guided trip wizard: departure/arrival from home, work, or any address, with Google-powered address autocomplete
Automatic distance calculation via the Google Distance Matrix API, one way or round trip
Configurable official mileage allowance scale, by vehicle type, fuel type, fiscal power and yearly distance
Correct differential calculation when an employee's yearly mileage crosses a scale threshold
Supports both cars and motorcycles as personal vehicles
Full audit trail: every counter update or reversal is logged on the employee's chatter
The yearly counter stays in sync automatically, even if an expense is reset to draft or refused
Integrates natively with the standard Odoo Expenses approval and accounting workflow

Setup Steps

  1. Install the module: hr_expense_ma
  2. Configure the Google Places API key in the general settings (required for address autocomplete and distance calculation)
  3. Mark the vehicles used by employees as Personal vehicle in Fleet, and assign a driver
  4. Enable Mileage allowance on the expense product used for mileage claims
  5. Configure at least one mileage allowance scale under Expenses > Configuration
  6. Employees create an expense with the mileage product, then click Enter Mileage Trip to fill in the trip and get the distance and amount computed automatically

Why this Module?

Reimbursing employees who use their personal vehicle for business trips usually means manually looking up an official mileage scale and computing the amount by hand — a process that is slow and error-prone, especially when an employee's yearly mileage crosses from one scale bracket to another mid-year.

This module removes that manual work: the employee only enters the trip (departure, arrival, vehicle), and Odoo takes care of the distance calculation, the scale lookup, and the reimbursement amount — including the correct differential amount when the yearly mileage total crosses a bracket threshold.

The mileage allowance scale is fully configurable, so it can be adapted to any official scale (French, Moroccan, or a custom internal policy), and the module is not tied to a specific country for the trip addresses themselves.
