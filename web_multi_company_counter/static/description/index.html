Multi-company selection counter

Know at a glance how many companies you have selected

Adds a small badge directly on the company switcher button, showing how many companies are currently selected, and lets you select a whole range of companies with a single shift+click.

Key features

Badge shown directly on the native company switcher button, not a separate icon
Only appears once 2 or more companies are selected: no clutter for single-company users
Updates live as soon as the company selection changes, no page reload needed
Shift+click range selection: select every company between two clicks at once
Range follows the list as displayed, including the switcher's search filter
Lightweight: only depends on Web, no configuration, Community and Enterprise

Setup steps

  1. Install the module: web_multi_company_counter
  2. Nothing else to configure: both features are available right away for every user with access to several companies

How to use

Selection counter

Tick 2 or more companies in the company switcher: the badge shows their number on the switcher button. Go back to a single company and the badge disappears.

Shift+click range selection

  1. Open the company switcher
  2. Click the checkbox of a first company
  3. Hold Shift and click the checkbox of another company, above or below

Every company in between (both included) is added to the selection. Companies you are not allowed to select are skipped. A click without Shift keeps Odoo's standard behaviour.

Why this module?

In multi-company setups, it is easy to lose track of how many companies are currently selected. Users sometimes think they are scoped to a single company while another one is still selected, leading to confusion about which records they are actually seeing.

This module adds a simple visual reminder right where users already look (the company switcher itself), and makes selecting many companies at once a matter of two clicks instead of one click per company.

Known limitations

  • A shift+click range only adds companies to the selection, it never unticks any
  • The starting point of a range is the last company clicked, remembered until the page is reloaded
