# Nuxly Multi-Company Selection Counter

Odoo 19 module adding a small badge next to the company selector in the systray,
showing how many companies are currently selected. Helps multi-company users
notice when they have more than one company selected at once, to avoid getting
confused by records shown from a company they did not expect.

It also adds shift+click range selection to the company switcher: click a
company, then shift+click another one further down the list, and every company
in between gets selected too — no need to click each one individually.
