From the trial balance (optionally filtered with its search bar), open
the general ledger of an account (the **General ledger** option of the
account line), then click **Next account** or **Previous account** to
review the following or preceding account of the trial balance list.

From the general ledger menu, type a search to filter the accounts, or
none to go through all of them, then click **Next account** (first
account of the list) or **Previous account** (last one).

A message is shown when there is no account further in that direction.
